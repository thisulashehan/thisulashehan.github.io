01 Research

Things I study

Two research areas and a steady habit of recording what I see outdoors.

Gastropod biodiversity

MAJOR FOCUS

Land and freshwater gastropods in Sri Lanka: how many species there are, how to tell them apart, and how they interact with their surroundings.

  • Species diversity
  • Taxonomic documentation
  • Ecological interactions
  • Conservation

Moth biodiversity

SECONDARY

Moth taxonomy and distribution in Sri Lanka, and what moths tell us about the ecosystems they live in.

  • Identification
  • Distribution mapping
  • Ecological significance

02 About

Curious about small animals.

2Research focuses
4Groups recorded
1Island, Sri Lanka

I am an undergraduate at the Faculty of Agriculture, University of Peradeniya. My interests are biodiversity and organismal biology, with a main focus on land and marine gastropods: their diversity, ecology, taxonomy and interactions with the environment.

I also study moth biodiversity, especially taxonomy, distribution and ecological significance in Sri Lanka. Through both areas I want to contribute to biodiversity documentation and conservation science.

Focus

Methods

Species identification, Taxonomic documentation, Distribution mapping, Diversity assessment
