About

I am an undergraduate at the Faculty of Agriculture, University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka. My academic interests are biodiversity and organismal biology, with a main focus on land and marine gastropods: their diversity, ecology, taxonomy and interactions with the environment.

I also study moth biodiversity, especially taxonomy, distribution and ecological significance in Sri Lanka. Through both areas I want to contribute to biodiversity documentation and conservation science.

Research

Major focus

Land and freshwater gastropod biodiversity in Sri Lanka

  • Species diversity assessment
  • Taxonomic documentation
  • Ecological interactions
  • Conservation relevance

Secondary focus

Moth biodiversity and taxonomy in Sri Lanka

  • Species identification
  • Distribution mapping
  • Ecological significance

Citizen science

I record field observations on iNaturalist, adding species records from across Sri Lanka. They cover arthropods, gastropods and other invertebrates, plus plants, and support biodiversity documentation and citizen science research.
