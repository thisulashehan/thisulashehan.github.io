About

I am an undergraduate at the Faculty of Agriculture, University of Peradeniya, Sri Lanka. My academic interests focus on biodiversity and organismal biology, with a primary research emphasis on land and marine gastropods. I am particularly interested in their diversity, ecology, taxonomy, and environmental interactions.

I also maintain a secondary research interest in moth biodiversity, especially their taxonomy, distribution, and ecological significance within Sri Lanka. Through these research areas, I aim to contribute to biodiversity documentation and conservation science.
